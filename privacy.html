Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Every Block Has a Story ("we," "us," "the site") is a place for people to share short stories about their neighborhoods and blocks. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the name and email address you provide. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never stored by us in plain text.

When you build out your profile, we store what you choose to add: a bio, a profile photo, and a banner image.

When you post a story, we store the video file, title, caption, location, and the IP address the submission came from. The IP address is used only for moderation and abuse prevention — it is not shown publicly.

To unlock story posting, we verify that you own an Every Block Has a Story product by checking your order against our Shopify store's order records (order number and, in some cases, email or ZIP code).

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and to show your posted stories and profile to other visitors.
  • To verify that you've purchased a product before allowing you to post a story.
  • To automatically screen submitted videos and profile photos for content that violates our Terms of Service (see "Automated Content Review" below).
  • To respond to messages sent through our Contact page.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

Automated Content Review

Videos and profile photos/banners you upload are automatically reviewed by an AI system (powered by Anthropic's Claude) to check for content that's inappropriate, off-topic, or violates our content guidelines, before they're shown publicly. This review looks only at the content itself (the image or video frame, and any title/caption/location you provided) — it isn't used to build a profile about you or shared for any other purpose.

Who We Share Information With

We don't sell your personal information. We do use a small number of service providers to run the site, and your information passes through them as part of normal operation:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication.
  • Cloudflare Stream — hosts and streams the videos you upload.
  • Shopify — processes purchases of our physical products and provides the order records we use to verify posting eligibility.
  • Anthropic — powers the automated content review described above.
  • Vercel — hosts the website and backend itself.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to keep you signed in between visits, and to remember things like which stories you've already viewed so we don't count duplicate views. This data stays on your device and is tied to your session, not sold or shared with advertisers.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account and posted content for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account or specific content deleted, contact us via the Contact page and we'll process your request.

Children's Privacy

Every Block Has a Story is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your Rights & Choices

You can edit or remove your bio, profile photo, and banner at any time from your profile page, and delete individual stories from your account page. For anything else — including a full account deletion or a copy of the data we hold about you — reach out through the Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach out through our Contact page.
